Evaluate the visual storytelling of the actual video.

## Prioritize

1. comprehension
2. subject visibility
3. hierarchy
4. pacing
5. visual interest
6. brand consistency
7. platform usability
8. production appropriateness

## Ask

- Does every significant visual addition earn its presence?
- Are numbers easier to understand?
- Is Chris still the visual anchor?
- Does anything feel cheap, synthetic, distracting, or generic?
- Does the visual treatment conflict with other videos in this week's slate?
- Does the week have enough variety?

Recommend changes only when they materially improve the content.
