# Brand Constitution — Chris Corallo

**Status:** Version 1 (seeded from Master Build Spec §2, Phase 0)
**Authority:** Level 3 in the authority hierarchy — below Human Owner and Hard Gates, above
Strategic Direction, Creative Director, and all specialists. See `/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` for
the full hierarchy and `/docs/DECISIONS.md` for how this file is versioned.

> This file defines what Chris and the brand **are**. It changes slowly, deliberately, and
> only through explicit human decision — never automatically as a side effect of performance
> data or a learning-system proposal. See "How this file changes" at the bottom.

---

## Identity

Chris Corallo is the umbrella personal brand.

Finance is currently the strongest expertise/skill pillar — but Chris must not become
permanently trapped as **"Finance Guy."**

- **Money Has Issues** is newsletter/editorial IP under Chris.
- **Monday Money Meetup** is an event/community property.

Potential franchises (not commitments — evaluated by the Creative Director as the brand
evolves):
- Show Me The Numbers
- Why Does Money Work Like This?
- Building Chris / build-in-public

## Audience

Primary psychographic:

> Ambitious people broadly in their 20s and 30s trying to get their shit together without
> pretending they already understand everything.

Psychographic relevance matters more than simplistic demographic targeting.

## Ethos

Working ethos:

> Stop pretending you understand shit you don't. Ask better questions, look at the actual
> numbers, and build a better life intentionally.

## Pillars

1. **Skill** — finance, money, debt, credit, investing, retirement, insurance,
   business/practical adulthood
2. **Mindset** — skepticism, ambition, decision-making, money psychology, growth
3. **Lifestyle** — career, business building, LA/life, gym, content journey, wins/failures
4. **Passions** — authentic interests that naturally become part of Chris's identity

## Principles

- Human > synthetic
- Specific > generic
- Interesting > merely informative
- Clear > sophisticated-sounding
- Personality + expertise > information alone
- Evidence > guru folklore
- Real numbers > vague claims
- Brand equity > temporary virality
- Original identity > copying creators
- Correct restraint > unnecessary production

## How this file changes

- Only the Human Owner (Chris) can edit this file's substance. Software may propose changes
  (via a `BeliefProposal` in Phase 7+) but never writes to it directly.
- Every substantive change is a new `BrandConstitutionVersion` row (see `/docs/DATA_MODEL.md`),
  never an in-place overwrite — the full version history is permanent and auditable.
- Nothing in this document is overridden by performance data. Historical success does not
  entitle any subsystem to narrow Chris's identity (see Brand Timeline in
  `/docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`).
