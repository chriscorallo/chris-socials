## Identity

A personality-driven brand about the absurd, surprising, relatable and sometimes uncomfortable
economics of everyday life.

Finance is the underlying expertise — but the content should not feel like traditional
financial education, and Chris must not become permanently trapped as "Finance Guy."

**Core positioning:** Entertainment that happens to teach people about money.

**Core creative promise:** "I investigate ridiculous questions about money and life, do the
actual math, and show you what the answers really mean."

**Core philosophy:** Finance is the tool used to answer an interesting question — not
necessarily the topic announced at the beginning. People arrive for curiosity and
entertainment, then stay for Chris's perspective, personality, intelligence and credibility.

The goal is not views. It is a recognizable brand people intentionally return to.

## Audience

Ambitious people in their twenties and thirties who want to improve their lives, understand
money, make better decisions — and be entertained.

Reach is not restricted to people already interested in finance. The broader audience is
anyone curious about: money and freedom, dating and relationships, lifestyle and status,
careers and earning, luxury and consumer behavior, human psychology, weird business models,
everyday decisions, hypothetical dilemmas, ridiculous calculations, the hidden costs of modern
life, and surprising economic consequences.

## What every video is for

Every video aims to deliver at least one of these reactions:

1. "Holy shit, I never thought about that."
2. "Holy shit, I can actually do that?"
3. "Holy shit, that's insane."
4. "Holy shit, that's literally me."
5. "Wait, which one is actually better?"
6. "I need to send this to someone."
7. "I want to see what this creator calculates next."

A useful video isn't automatically entertaining; an entertaining video isn't automatically
valuable; a viral video isn't automatically good at earning followers. The job is to combine
entertainment, usefulness, credibility and brand connection.

## The series

Recognizable, rotating formats (the calendar rotates them rather than repeating one):

- **Pick Your Poison** — financial dilemmas with two or more defensible choices. The viewer
  decides first; the middle challenges that decision; the ending invites them to defend or
  reconsider. Never invent one "correct" answer when it depends on preferences or risk.
- **I Did The Math** — entertaining investigations of questions people rarely calculate,
  with escalating calculations and an unexpected payoff.
- **The Financial Butterfly Effect** — how one decision plays out over years or decades. Never
  present hypothetical returns as guaranteed.
- **How Broke Is This Character?** — could fictional characters afford their lives? Clearly
  separate fictional assumptions from documented facts.
- **Money Makes No Sense** — counterintuitive economics and business behavior: a mystery,
  then the hidden incentive.
- **The Cost of Being Alive** — the hidden economics of normal human behavior. Relatable and
  funny — never stereotyping or shaming people for ordinary spending.

## Success is a relationship, not a number

The audience journey: attention → curiosity → retention → payoff → brand connection → follow.
A video with fewer views but stronger relevant follower conversion (new followers per 1,000
views) can be worth more for the brand — but follower conversion is never optimized in
isolation from reach, audience relevance, repeat viewing and quality.

## Principles

- Interesting question > announced topic
- Entertainment that teaches > education that tries to entertain
- Real evidence and real math > guru folklore
- Specific > generic; clear > sophisticated-sounding
- A satisfying payoff > a fake twist
- Provocative is fine; misleading is not. Controversy is not originality.
- Trust > engagement; brand equity > temporary virality
- Never promise virality — build the strongest work, test it against real behavior, learn,
  improve

When in doubt, prioritize: an interesting question, a compelling opening, a developing story,
real evidence, a satisfying payoff, a recognizable creator perspective, a legitimate reason to
return.
