My friend got a $10,000 raise last year. Six months later, he had less money and savings than before the raise. How does that even happen? What happened is lifestyle creep. The new car payment was $400 a month. The nicer apartment was another $300 a month. So the raise was gone before it ever hit his account. Here's the fix. First, decide where the race goes before the first paycheck lands. Second, automate half of it into savings. Third, spend the other half guilt-free, do that, and the race actually makes you richer. Follow for more money stuff that actually makes sense.
